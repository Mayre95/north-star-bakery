About North Star Bakery

Our Story

North Star Bakery began in a small home kitchen, where our founder baked bread for neighbors every weekend. As word spread, the weekend loaves turned into a storefront on Maple Street. Today we are still a family-run bakery that believes good bread brings a community together.

Our Sourcing Philosophy

We buy flour from a regional mill, eggs and butter from family farms, and fruit from local growers whenever it is in season. Buying locally keeps our ingredients fresh, supports our neighbors, and reduces the distance our food travels.
